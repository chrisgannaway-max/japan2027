APPENDIX B

Planning checklist

Book first

  • International flights and open-jaw return from Osaka
  • Disney hotel with eligible early-entry benefit
  • DisneySea admission for the selected date
  • Shibuya Sky sunset slot
  • teamLab Planets
  • Sumo experience

Later reservations

  • Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka hotels
  • Capsule hotel for one night
  • Shinkansen seats on the Mt. Fuji side
  • Tea ceremony
  • Kobe beef dinner only if selected

Trip notes

  • Friday disappears while flying west; arrive Saturday
  • Returning east, you can leave Japan Sunday and reach the U.S. Sunday
  • Early April may overlap cherry-blossom season, but bloom timing varies
  • Use luggage forwarding before the bullet-train leg
